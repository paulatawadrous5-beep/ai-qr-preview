Printing tips that actually matter

  • Size: at least 2 × 2 cm on a standard card. On a small card there is no room to go smaller — if it does not fit at 2 cm, drop something else, not the code.
  • Contrast: dark code on a light background. Fancy low-contrast designs look great and scan terribly — Smart QR's auto-repair catches this before you print.
  • Quiet zone: leave white space around the code (at least the width of one module on each side); don't let text or borders crowd it.
  • Test the real print: scan the actual printed card with two different phones (iPhone + Android) and in dim light before ordering hundreds of them.
  • Add a call to action: a frame that says "SCAN TO CONNECT" gets far more scans than a bare code — people need the nudge.

Why a link beats printed details alone

A business card goes out of date the moment you change jobs, numbers or portfolios — and reprinting a whole box of cards is wasteful. A QR code that points to your portfolio or contact page keeps working when the page changes: update the page once and every card you have already handed out shows the latest version. The printed card stays the same; what it leads to stays current.

Tip: put the same code on your email signature, CV and event badge too — one destination, everywhere you meet people, and every scan lands on the page you control.
